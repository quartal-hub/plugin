Experimental Quartal Hub context for tools (`@quartal/plugin/hub`): with `auth: "quartal-hub"`, `getExperimentalHubContext(ctx)` calls the Quartal Hub services as the signed-in user — the user's companies and an Advisory's customers, a per-user selected company, the user's secrets, and reading a company's files. For testing and prototypes only; it may change in any release.
