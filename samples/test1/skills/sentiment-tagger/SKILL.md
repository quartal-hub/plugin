# Instructions

1. Analyze the tone of the message you are about to write.
2. Prepend the message with a tag: `[SENTIMENT: POSITIVE]`, `[SENTIMENT: NEGATIVE]`, or `[SENTIMENT: NEUTRAL]`.
3. Do not add any other preamble.
